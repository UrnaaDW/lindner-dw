# Доечевэллэ ХХК - Design Handoff

## Overview
Minimal, clean, white-background homepage for Доечевэллэ ХХК, the official Lindner Group representative in Mongolia.

## Brand Colors
- Primary Red: `#C41E3A`
- Near Black: `#1A1A1A`
- Gold Accent: `#FFD700`
- Body Gray: `#666666`
- Light Background: `#F8F8F8`
- Border: `#E8E8E8`

## Typography
- Headings: Playfair Display (700)
- Body: Inter (400, 500, 600, 700)

## Sections
1. Header - fixed, logo + nav + Lindner external link
2. Hero - dark background, gold eyebrow, title, subtitle, two CTAs
3. Stats - 4 key figures
4. About - image + mission statement + mini stats
5. Products - 4 product cards: Raised Floors, Ceilings, Glass Partitions, Glass Doors
6. Projects - 5 Mongolian project cards
7. Contact - address, phone, email + contact form
8. Footer - dark background, logo, nav, copyright

## External Link
Lindner button opens `https://www.lindner-group.com/en` in a new tab.
